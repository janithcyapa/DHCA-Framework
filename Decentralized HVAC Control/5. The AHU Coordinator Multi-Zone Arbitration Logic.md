# 5. The AHU Coordinator — Multi-Zone Arbitration Logic (Updated)

## Overview

The AHU Coordinator receives one Ideal-Ask bundle per zone each timestep:

$$\mathcal{I}_i = \{T_{s,i}^*,\;W_{s,i}^*,\;C_{s,i}^*,\;u_{cmd,i},\;S_i\}$$

and solves a single QP to find the physical AHU commands $[T_{cc},\;T_{hc},\;\gamma]^T$ that best serve all zones simultaneously. The result is rate-smoothed before being written to EnergyPlus actuators.

---

## 1. Decision Variables

$$U_{AHU} = \begin{bmatrix} T_{cc} \\ T_{hc} \\ \gamma \end{bmatrix}$$

* $T_{cc}$: cooling coil leaving-air temperature (°C).
* $T_{hc}$: heating coil leaving-air temperature (°C).
* $\gamma$: outdoor-air fraction ($0 \le \gamma \le 1$).

**Supply air equations (unchanged):**

$$T_s = T_{hc} + \Delta T_{fan}, \qquad W_s \approx \kappa\,T_{cc} + W_{offset}$$

where $\kappa$ and $W_{offset}$ are from a first-order Taylor expansion of $w_{sat}(T_{cc})$ around the previous operating point $T_{cc}^{prev}$, and $\Delta T_{fan}$ is an adaptive fan heat-rise estimate updated online:

$$\hat{\Delta T}_{fan} \leftarrow 0.99\,\hat{\Delta T}_{fan} + 0.01\,(T_{fan,out} - T_{hc,out})$$

---

## 2. Zone Weights

Each zone's contribution is weighted by its flow and saturation:

$$\omega_i = q_T \cdot (u_{cmd,i})^2 \cdot S_i^3, \qquad \psi_i = q_W \cdot (u_{cmd,i})^2 \cdot S_i^3, \qquad \chi_i = q_C \cdot (u_{cmd,i})^2 \cdot S_i^3$$

Note: the saturation exponent is **cubed** ($S_i^3$) rather than linear, which sharpens the contrast between a zone near its flow ceiling and one running at mid-range — a more aggressive form of priority weighting than a linear or squared scheme.

**Coordinator weights:**

| Symbol | Value |
|--------|-------|
| $q_T$ | $1.0$ |
| $q_W$ | $10^8$ |
| $q_C$ | $10^{-2}$ |
| $\phi_c$ (cooling energy) | $0.1$ |
| $\phi_h$ (heating energy) | $0.1$ |
| $\phi_v$ (ventilation energy) | $10.0$ |

---

## 3. Objective Function

$$\min_{T_{cc},T_{hc},\gamma}\; J = J_{track} + J_{energy}$$

### 3.1 Controllability Tracking ($J_{track}$)

Each of the three AHU actuators tracks a **different** aggregate ask:

* **$T_{cc}$ tracks humidity** (via linearised $w_{sat}$): $W_s \approx \kappa T_{cc} + W_{offset}$
* **$T_{hc}$ tracks temperature** (with fan heat-rise offset): $T_s = T_{hc} + \hat{\Delta T}_{fan}$
* **$\gamma$ tracks CO₂ dilution**: each zone's CO₂ ask $C_{s,i}^*$ informs how much outdoor air is needed

$$J_{track} = \underbrace{\sum_i \psi_i(W_s - W_{s,i}^*)^2}_{\text{humidity: drives }T_{cc}} + \underbrace{\sum_i \omega_i(T_s - T_{s,i}^*)^2}_{\text{temperature: drives }T_{hc}} + \underbrace{\sum_i \chi_i(C_{mix} - C_{s,i}^*)^2}_{\text{CO}_2\text{: drives }\gamma}$$

Substituting the linearisations and expanding into standard QP form yields aggregates:

$$\Sigma_\psi = \sum_i \psi_i, \quad \Sigma_\omega = \sum_i \omega_i, \quad \Sigma_\chi = \sum_i \chi_i$$
$$\Sigma_{\psi W^*} = \sum_i \psi_i W_{s,i}^*, \quad \Sigma_{\omega T^*} = \sum_i \omega_i T_{s,i}^*, \quad \Sigma_{\chi C^*} = \sum_i \chi_i C_{s,i}^*$$

### 3.2 Energy Minimisation ($J_{energy}$)

Thermodynamic proxy costs penalise mechanical effort without computing kW directly:

$$J_{energy} = \phi_c(T_{mix} - T_{cc})^2 + \phi_h(T_{hc} - T_{cc})^2 + \phi_v\,\gamma^2$$

$$T_{mix} = \gamma T_{out} + (1-\gamma)T_{ret}, \quad C_{mix} = \gamma C_{out} + (1-\gamma)C_{ret}$$

---

## 4. QP Matrix Assembly

**Decision vector:** $x = [T_{cc},\;T_{hc},\;\gamma]^T$

### Hessian $P$

$$P_{11} = 2\,\Sigma_\psi\kappa^2 + 2\phi_c + 2\phi_h$$
$$P_{22} = 2\,\Sigma_\omega + 2\phi_h$$
$$P_{33} = 2\phi_c(T_{out} - T_{ret})^2 + 2\,\Sigma_\chi(C_{ret} - C_{out})^2 + 2\phi_v$$
$$P_{12} = P_{21} = -2\phi_h$$
$$P_{13} = P_{31} = -2\phi_c(T_{out} - T_{ret})$$

(all other off-diagonals are zero; $P += 10^{-6}I$ for regularisation)

### Gradient $q$

$$q_1 = 2\,\Sigma_\psi\kappa W_{offset} - 2\kappa\,\Sigma_{\psi W^*} - 2\phi_c T_{ret}$$
$$q_2 = 2\,\Sigma_\omega\hat{\Delta T}_{fan} - 2\,\Sigma_{\omega T^*}$$
$$q_3 = 2\phi_c T_{ret}(T_{out} - T_{ret}) - 2(C_{ret} - C_{out})\!\left(\Sigma_\chi C_{ret} - \Sigma_{\chi C^*}\right)$$

---

## 5. Constraints

All six constraints are **hard** (no slack variables at the coordinator level):

### Constraint Matrix $A$ and Bounds $[l,\;u]$

$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & (T_{ret}-T_{out}) \\ -1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & (C_{out}-C_{ret}) \end{bmatrix}, \quad l = \begin{bmatrix} T_{coil,min} \\ T_{coil,min} \\ \gamma_{min} \\ -\infty \\ 0 \\ -\infty \end{bmatrix}, \quad u = \begin{bmatrix} T_{coil,max} \\ T_{coil,max} \\ 1.0 \\ T_{ret} \\ +\infty \\ C_{max} - C_{ret} \end{bmatrix}$$

**Physical limits:**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| $T_{coil,min}$ | $5.0^\circ\text{C}$ |
| $T_{coil,max}$ | $40.0^\circ\text{C}$ |
| $\gamma_{min}$ | $0.04$ (OA\_MIN/OA\_MAX = 0.1/2.5) |
| $C_{max}$ | $1000\ \text{ppm}$ |

**Constraint semantics:**

| Row | Name                    | Description                                                                                |
| --- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Cooling coil box        | $T_{coil,min} \le T_{cc} \le T_{coil,max}$                                                 |
| 2   | Heating coil box        | $T_{coil,min} \le T_{hc} \le T_{coil,max}$                                                 |
| 3   | OA fraction box         | $\gamma_{min} \le \gamma \le 1.0$                                                          |
| 4   | **Cooling restriction** | $T_{cc} + \gamma(T_{ret}-T_{out}) \le T_{ret}$ — coil cannot heat the mixed stream         |
| 5   | **Heating restriction** | $T_{hc} \ge T_{cc}$ — heating coil downstream of cooling coil                              |
| 6   | **CO₂ safety ceiling**  | $\gamma(C_{out}-C_{ret}) \le C_{max}-C_{ret}$ — forces damper open when return CO₂ is high |
|     |                         |                                                                                            |

---

## 6. Output Mapping

From the solved $[T_{cc}^*,\;T_{hc}^*,\;\gamma^*]$:

| Actuator | Formula | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| Cooling coil setpoint | $T_{cc}^*$ | Written directly to `CC_Temp_SP` |
| Heating coil / SAT setpoint | $T_{hc}^*$ | Written to `HC_Temp_SP` **and** `SAT_Sch_SP` |
| OA flow setpoint | $\gamma^* \times \text{OA}_{max}$ | $\text{OA}_{max} = 2.5\ \text{kg/s}$ |
| Humidifier / coil humidity SP | $w_{sat}(T_{cc}^*)$ | Written to `Hum_Sch_SP`, `CC_Hum_SP`, `CC_Hum_Max_SP` |

The humidifier setpoint is derived from the saturation curve at the cooling-coil temperature: this is the physically achievable minimum humidity ratio the coil can produce at that temperature, which bounds the dehumidification capability without reheat.

---

## 7. Setpoint Smoothing

Before writing to hardware, each command is rate-limited via a blended EMA + hard-clamp:

$$\text{blended} = \alpha\,x_{new} + (1-\alpha)\,x_{prev}$$
$$\text{smoothed} = x_{prev} + \text{clip}(\text{blended} - x_{prev},\;-\Delta_{max},\;+\Delta_{max})$$

| Parameter | $T_{cc}$ | $T_{hc}$ | $\gamma$ |
|-----------|----------|----------|----------|
| $\alpha$ (blend) | 0.4 | 0.4 | 0.4 |
| $\Delta_{max}$ (max step) | $1.5^\circ\text{C}$ | $1.5^\circ\text{C}$ | $0.1$ |

If OSQP fails, previous smoothed setpoints are held unchanged (no fallback to neutral — stability is better served by holding the last known-good state than jumping to an arbitrary default).

---

## 8. Actuation in EnergyPlus

The `on_inside_hvac_system_iteration_loop` callback applies the coordinator commands each timestep:

* `OA_Flow_SP` ← $\gamma^* \times \text{OA}_{max}$ (absolute mass-flow kg/s)
* `CC_Temp_SP` ← $T_{cc}^*$
* `HC_Temp_SP` and `SAT_Sch_SP` ← $T_{hc}^*$
* `Hum_Sch_SP`, `CC_Hum_SP`, `CC_Hum_Max_SP` ← $w_{sat}(T_{cc}^*)$

**Reheater suppression:** all zone-level reheat actuators (`{zone}_Reheat_SP`) are forced to $10^\circ\text{C}$ each timestep, disabling the reheating coils entirely. This enforces the no-reheat design constraint and prevents EnergyPlus's built-in VAV reheat logic from interfering with the custom controller.

**Dew-point actuation split (bug fix):** an earlier version re-derived and clamped the supply-air setpoint to the cooling-coil dew point at every timestep, silently overriding `HC_Temp_SP` and pinning the delivered air near $\sim 10^\circ\text{C}$ regardless of the coordinator's intent. The fix separates the two setpoints: `CC_Temp_SP` governs the cooling coil (and inherits the dew-point physics naturally), while `HC_Temp_SP` / `SAT_Sch_SP` receives the coordinator's unmodified $T_{hc}^*$ command.

---

## 9. Design Notes and Known Limitations

**No slack variables:** all six coordinator constraints are hard. The coordinator relies on the zone-level Ideal-Ask QP to produce feasible requests; if return air states are extreme the CO₂ or cooling constraints can make the problem tight, but OSQP's interior-point method handles near-degenerate cases gracefully given the energy regularisation terms.

**CO₂ tracking vs. ventilation penalty trade-off:** $\phi_v = 10.0$ is deliberately large relative to $q_C = 10^{-2}$. The solver will suppress outdoor-air damper opening unless the CO₂ safety constraint (row 6) forces it — this represents free-cooling and IAQ as competing signals mediated by the constraint rather than by weight tuning alone.

**Humidity and temperature cross-coupling:** $T_{cc}$ jointly controls dehumidification (via $w_{sat}$) and contributes to the mixed-air temperature via $\phi_c$, while $T_{hc}$ reheats the air for thermal comfort. The coupling terms $P_{12}$ and $P_{13}$ in the Hessian encode this physically — the QP can autonomously discover that raising $\gamma$ (free cooling) lets it raise $T_{cc}$ (less dehumidification, less reheat) when the outdoor air is dry and cool.

**Zone floor vs. coordinator floor:** the zone Ideal-Ask QP enforces $T_{s,min} = 10^\circ\text{C}$; the coordinator allows $T_{cc}$ down to $5^\circ\text{C}$. The coordinator's feasible window is a superset of what any zone will request — intentional, to preserve margin for future zones or edge cases.
