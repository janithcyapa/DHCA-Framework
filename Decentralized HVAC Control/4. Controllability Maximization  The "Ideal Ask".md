# 4. Controllability Maximisation — The "Ideal Ask" (Updated)

## 4.1 Overview: Sequential Linearisation (Unchanged)

The bilinearity of $\dot{Q} = \rho_{air} c_p\, u\,(T_s - T_{in})$ prevents solving for $(u, T_s)$ simultaneously in a standard QP. The solution is the two-phase sequential scheme:

- **Phase 1** — fix $v_s = [T_s, W_s, C_s]^T$ as constants; solve the flow MPC for $u^*$ (Section 3).
- **Phase 2** — fix $u = u^*$ (the final, smoothed, clipped command actually sent to the VAV); the bilinear term becomes linear in the AHU supply request $Z = [T_s, W_s, C_s]^T$.

Phase 2 is called the **Ideal Ask** and is solved as a secondary QP.

---

## 4.2 Dynamic Neutral Targets

Before solving, a zone-local "neutral" supply state is estimated from the mixed-air composition. This replaces the fixed `W_neutral = 0.015` used in early drafts and prevents the ask from requesting an unnecessarily extreme condition when the zone is already comfortable.

**Step 1 — estimate outdoor-air fraction:**

$$\hat\gamma = \text{clip}\!\left(\frac{C_{in} - C_s}{C_{in} - C_{out}},\; 0,\; 1\right) \quad \text{if } |C_{in} - C_{out}| > 10\ \text{ppm, else } 0.1$$

**Step 2 — mixed-air states:**

$$T_{mix} = \hat\gamma\,T_{out} + (1-\hat\gamma)\,T_{in}, \qquad W_{mix} = \hat\gamma\,W_{out} + (1-\hat\gamma)\,W_{in}$$

**Step 3 — neutral targets (clipped to AHU supply limits):**

$$T_{neutral} = \text{clip}(T_{mix},\;T_{s,min},\;T_{s,max})$$
$$W_{neutral} = \text{clip}(W_{mix},\;W_{s,min},\;W_{s,max})$$
$$C_{neutral} = C_{in} \quad \text{(current zone CO2 — asking for more dilution only when needed)}$$

## 4.3 Phase-2 Linearisation

With $u_0^*$ frozen, re-evaluate the full non-linear dynamics at $(x_0, u_0^*, v_{s,0})$:

$$f_{ask} = f(x_0,\, u_0^*,\, v_{s,0})$$

Derive the continuous Jacobians with respect to states ($A_{c,ask}$) and supply inputs ($B_{ask}$):

$$A_{c,ask} = \begin{bmatrix}
\beta_{air}(-\alpha_{ext}-\alpha_{int}-\rho c_p u_0^*) & \beta_{air}\,\alpha_{int} & 0 & 0 \\
\beta_{mass}\,\alpha_{int} & -\beta_{mass}\,\alpha_{int} & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & -\beta_{air}\,c_p\,\rho\,u_0^* & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & -\beta_{air}\,\rho\,c_p\,u_0^*
\end{bmatrix}$$

$$B_{ask} = \begin{bmatrix}
\beta_{air}\,\rho\,c_p\,u_0^* & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & \beta_{air}\,c_p\,\rho\,u_0^* & 0 \\
0 & 0 & \beta_{air}\,\rho\,c_p\,u_0^*
\end{bmatrix}$$

Continuous affine offset: $c_{c,ask} = f_{ask} - A_{c,ask}\,x_0 - B_{ask}\,v_{s,0}$

Forward-Euler discretisation at timestep $\Delta t$:

$$A_{d,ask} = I + A_{c,ask}\,\Delta t, \qquad B_{d,ask} = B_{ask}\,\Delta t, \qquad c_{d,ask} = c_{c,ask}\,\Delta t$$

## 4.4 Horizon Prediction with $Z$ Held Constant

Unlike Phase 1 where $U$ is an $N$-step trajectory, $Z = [T_s, W_s, C_s]^T$ is a **single constant triple** held over the $N$-step horizon. This keeps the ask self-consistent with reality (the AHU delivers one set of conditions, not a per-minute schedule) and makes the input map collapse to a convenient form:

$$\Theta_{ask} = \Phi_{ask}\,B_{d,ask} \in \mathbb{R}^{4N \times 3}, \qquad g_{ask} = \Psi_{ask}\,x_0 + \Phi_{ask}\,c_{d,ask} \in \mathbb{R}^{4N}$$

where $\Psi_{ask}$ and $\Phi_{ask}$ are the standard power-sum matrices built from $A_{d,ask}$.

State prediction trajectories:

$$T_{1:N} = F_T\,Z + g_T, \quad W_{1:N} = F_W\,Z + g_W, \quad C_{1:N} = F_C\,Z + g_C$$

with $F_{T/W/C} = S_{T/W/C}\,\Theta_{ask}$ and $g_{T/W/C} = S_{T/W/C}\,g_{ask}$.

## 4.5 Phase-2 QP

**Decision vector:** $z = [T_s,\;W_s,\;C_s,\;\epsilon_T{}_{N},\;\epsilon_W{}_{N},\;\epsilon_C{}_{N}]^T \in \mathbb{R}^{3+3N}$

### Objective

Minimise deviation from the dynamic neutral targets while keeping zone states in bounds:

$$J_{ask} = q_T(T_s - T_{neutral})^2 + q_W(W_s - W_{neutral})^2 + q_C(C_s - C_{neutral})^2 + \lambda_T\|\epsilon_T\|^2 + \lambda_W\|\epsilon_W\|^2 + \lambda_C\|\epsilon_C\|^2$$

**Hessian:**

$$H_{ask} = 2\,\text{diag}\bigl([q_T,\;q_W,\;q_C,\;\underbrace{\lambda_T,\ldots}_N,\;\underbrace{\lambda_W,\ldots}_N,\;\underbrace{\lambda_C,\ldots}_N]\bigr) + 10^{-6}I$$

**Gradient:**

$$f_{ask} = \bigl[-2q_T T_{neutral},\;-2q_W W_{neutral},\;-2q_C C_{neutral},\;\mathbf{0}_{3N}\bigr]^T$$

**Weights:**

| Weight | Value |
|--------|-------|
| $q_T$ | $1.0$ |
| $q_W$ | $10^6$ |
| $q_C$ | $10^{-3}$ |
| $\lambda_{T,W,C}$ | shared with Phase-1 ($10^3,\;10^{10},\;10^1$) |

### Constraints

$$A_{ask,con} = \begin{bmatrix}
F_T & -I_N & 0 & 0 \\
-F_T & -I_N & 0 & 0 \\
F_W & 0 & -I_N & 0 \\
-F_W & 0 & -I_N & 0 \\
F_C & 0 & 0 & -I_N \\
0 & I_N & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & I_N & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & I_N \\
I_3 & 0_{3\times N} & 0_{3\times N} & 0_{3\times N}
\end{bmatrix}$$

| Block | $l$ | $u$ | Constraint |
|-------|-----|-----|------------|
| 1–2 | $-\infty$ | $\pm(T_{max/min}\mathbf{1} \mp g_T)$ | Soft temperature bounds |
| 3–4 | $-\infty$ | $\pm(W_{max/min}\mathbf{1} \mp g_W)$ | Soft humidity bounds |
| 5 | $-\infty$ | $C_{max}\mathbf{1} - g_C$ | Soft CO₂ ceiling |
| 6–8 | $0$ | $+\infty$ | $\epsilon \ge 0$ |
| 9 | $[T_{s,min},\;W_{s,min},\;C_{s,min}]$ | $[T_{s,max},\;W_{s,max},\;C_{s,max}]$ | **AHU physical box** |

**AHU supply limits used in Phase-2:**

| Variable | Floor | Ceiling |
|----------|-------|---------|
| $T_s$ | $10.0^\circ\text{C}$ | $40.0^\circ\text{C}$ |
| $W_s$ | $w_{sat}(10.0^\circ\text{C})$ | $0.015\ \text{kg/kg}$ |
| $C_s$ | $420\ \text{ppm}$ | $1000\ \text{ppm}$ |

Note: $T_{s,min} = 10^\circ\text{C}$ at the zone level is stricter than the coordinator's coil floor of $5^\circ\text{C}$; the coordinator's feasible set is a superset of what any zone will request.

## 4.6 Flow-Authority Taper

**Problem:** at low VAV flow, the zone has little thermal authority. A small temperature drift would previously cause the ask to snap to $T_{s,min}$ or $T_{s,max}$, injecting a wildly extreme request into the coordinator despite negligible actual delivery. This produced limit-cycle chatter when the coordinator responded, the zone briefly corrected, MPC reopened the damper, the coil resumed, and the cycle repeated.

**Solution — flow-authority taper:** the solved ask $Z^*$ is blended toward the neutral point $Z_{neutral}$ based on how much flow authority is currently in use:

$$\text{taper} = \text{clip}\!\left(\frac{u_{cmd} - u_{min,vent}}{u_{max} - u_{min,vent}},\;0,\;1\right)$$

$$T_s^{*,\text{tapered}} = \text{taper}\cdot T_s^* + (1-\text{taper})\cdot T_{neutral}$$

and identically for $W_s^*$ and $C_s^*$.

When $u_{cmd} \approx u_{min,vent}$ (ventilation-only mode), taper → 0 and the ask collapses to neutral. When $u_{cmd} \approx u_{max}$ (full saturation), taper → 1 and the full QP result is broadcast.

The ventilation minimum was raised from $\sim 1\%$ to $7.5\%$ of $u_{max}$ ($u_{min,vent} \approx 0.15\ \text{m}^3\text{/s}$) for a more physically realistic dead-band floor.

---

## 4.7 Saturation Index

$$S_i = \frac{u_{cmd}}{u_{max}} \in [0,\;1]$$

This is broadcast alongside the ideal ask to the AHU Coordinator, which uses it to weight each zone's demand proportionally in the multi-zone arbitration QP.

---

## 4.8 Fallback

If the Phase-2 QP fails (OSQP non-solved status), `_fallback_ideal_ask()` applies a proportional/bang-bang heuristic based on $(T_{in} - T_{ref})$, $(W_{in} - W_{ref})$, and the CO₂ trend $\Delta C_{in}$. The taper is **not** applied to the fallback — it only modifies the QP result.
