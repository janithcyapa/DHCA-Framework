# 3. Actuation-Minimizing Deadband Zone MPC (Updated)

## The Control Philosophy

Unlike traditional MPC which minimises state-tracking errors $(x_k - x_{ref})^2$, this Economic / Deadband MPC removes state tracking entirely from the objective. The objective is strictly to **minimise VAV flow rate ($u$) and its rate of change ($\Delta u$)**, subject to keeping the states inside a specified comfort band. To prevent solver failure during extreme disturbances the state bounds are formulated as **soft constraints** heavily penalised by slack variables ($\epsilon$).

An additional **quadratic centering cost** is layered onto the flow objective. This pulls the predicted temperature toward $T_{ref}$ and predicted humidity toward the midpoint $\tfrac{W_{max}+W_{min}}{2}$ inside the deadband, so the zone does not drift to an extreme before flow correction is triggered.

---

## Objective Function (Cost)

$$J = \underbrace{\sum_{k=0}^{N-1} \Big( r\,u_k^2 + r_\Delta(u_k - u_{k-1})^2 \Big)}_{\text{flow minimisation}} + \underbrace{q_T \sum_{k=1}^{N}(T_{in,k} - T_{ref})^2 + q_W \sum_{k=1}^{N}(W_{in,k} - W_{ref})^2}_{\text{centering}} + \underbrace{\sum_{k=1}^{N} \Big( \lambda_T \epsilon_{T,k}^2 + \lambda_W \epsilon_{W,k}^2 + \lambda_C \epsilon_{C,k}^2 \Big)}_{\text{soft constraint violation}}$$

where $W_{ref} = \tfrac{W_{max}+W_{min}}{2}$ and the penalty hierarchy enforces $\lambda_{T,W,C} \gg r$, with humidity treated as stricter than temperature in tuning ($\lambda_W \gg \lambda_T$).

**Horizon and Tuning Parameters**

| Parameter | Value | Role |
|-----------|-------|------|
| $N$ | 20 steps | Prediction horizon |
| $r$ | $10^{-1}$ | Absolute flow penalty |
| $r_\Delta$ | $5 \times 10^{1}$ | Slew-rate penalty |
| $\lambda_T$ | $10^{3}$ | Temperature soft-constraint weight |
| $\lambda_W$ | $10^{10}$ | Humidity soft-constraint weight |
| $\lambda_C$ | $10^{1}$ | CO₂ soft-constraint weight |
| $q_T$ (centering) | $5.0$ | Temperature centering weight |
| $q_W$ (centering) | $10^{7}$ | Humidity centering weight |
| $\mu_T$ (linear) | $10^4$ | Linear penalty on $\epsilon_T$ (asymmetric push toward centre) |
| $\Delta u_{max}$ | $0.05\ \text{m}^3\text{/s}$ per step | Hard slew-rate cap |

## Linearisation & Prediction

The derivation in the original document (Taylor expansion → affine form → Forward Euler discretisation → horizon matrices $\Psi, \Theta, \Phi$) is unchanged. The continuous Jacobians $A_c$ and $B_c$ are evaluated at the EKF operating point $(x_0, u_0, d_0)$ each timestep.

Prediction:

$$X = \Psi x_0 + \Theta U + \Phi c_d$$

Extraction matrices $S_T, S_W, S_C$ yield:

$$T_{1:N} = F_T U + g_T, \quad W_{1:N} = F_W U + g_W, \quad C_{1:N} = F_C U + g_C$$

## QP Assembly (Updated)

### Decision Vector

$$z = \begin{bmatrix} U_{N\times 1} \\ \epsilon_T{}_{N\times 1} \\ \epsilon_W{}_{N\times 1} \\ \epsilon_C{}_{N\times 1} \end{bmatrix}_{4N \times 1}$$

### Hessian $H$ (with Centering)

The centering terms $q_T \|F_T U + g_T - T_{ref}\|^2$ and $q_W \|F_W U + g_W - W_{ref}\|^2$ contribute quadratic terms in $U$:

$$H_{UU} = 2\Big(rI + D^T r_\Delta D + q_T F_T^T F_T + q_W F_W^T F_W\Big)$$

$$H = 2\begin{bmatrix} H_{UU} & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & \lambda_T I & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & \lambda_W I & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \lambda_C I \end{bmatrix} + 10^{-6}I \quad \text{(regularisation)}$$

### Linear Cost $f$

The centering terms also contribute linear terms in $U$ (the cross term between $F_T U$ and the constant residual $g_T - T_{ref}$):

$$f_U = -2 r_\Delta D^T E\,u_{prev} + 2 q_T F_T^T (g_T - T_{ref}\mathbf{1}) + 2 q_W F_W^T (g_W - W_{ref}\mathbf{1})$$

$$f = \begin{bmatrix} f_U \\ \mu_T \mathbf{1}_N \\ \mathbf{0}_N \\ \mathbf{0}_N \end{bmatrix}$$

Note: the linear term $\mu_T \mathbf{1}$ on $\epsilon_T$ creates an asymmetric cost that further discourages temperature violations beyond the quadratic slack penalty.

### Constraint Matrix $A_{ineq}$ (Two-Sided OSQP Format)

All constraints are expressed in the two-sided form $l \le A z \le u$:

$$A_{con} = \begin{bmatrix} F_T & -I_N & 0 & 0 \\ -F_T & -I_N & 0 & 0 \\ F_W & 0 & -I_N & 0 \\ -F_W & 0 & -I_N & 0 \\ F_C & 0 & 0 & -I_N \\ 0 & I_N & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & I_N & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & I_N \\ I_N & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ D & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$

**Bounds (10 blocks of size $N$):**

| Block | $l$ | $u$ | Constraint |
|-------|-----|-----|------------|
| 1 | $-\infty$ | $T_{max}\mathbf{1} - g_T$ | $F_T U - \epsilon_T \le T_{max} - g_T$ (temp upper) |
| 2 | $-\infty$ | $-T_{min}\mathbf{1} + g_T$ | $-F_T U - \epsilon_T \le -T_{min} + g_T$ (temp lower) |
| 3 | $-\infty$ | $W_{max}\mathbf{1} - g_W$ | $F_W U - \epsilon_W \le W_{max} - g_W$ (humidity upper) |
| 4 | $-\infty$ | $-W_{min}\mathbf{1} + g_W$ | humidity lower |
| 5 | $-\infty$ | $C_{max}\mathbf{1} - g_C$ | CO₂ ceiling |
| 6–8 | $0$ | $+\infty$ | $\epsilon_{T,W,C} \ge 0$ (slack non-negativity) |
| 9 | $u_{min}\mathbf{1}$ | $u_{max}\mathbf{1}$ | VAV actuator limits |
| 10 | $-\Delta u_{max}\mathbf{1} + Eu_{prev}$ | $+\Delta u_{max}\mathbf{1} + Eu_{prev}$ | **Hard slew-rate constraint (new)** |

The slew-rate is now a **hard constraint** (block 10) rather than purely a penalty, preventing large step changes even when the solver is under pressure from soft-constraint violations.

## Zone Limits

| Variable | Min | Max | Notes |
|----------|-----|-----|-------|
| $T_{in}$ | $T_{ref} - 1^\circ\text{C}$ | $T_{ref} + 1^\circ\text{C}$ | Deadband ±1 °C around per-zone setpoint |
| $W_{in}$ | $W(T_{in}, \text{RH}=30\%)$ | $W(T_{in}, \text{RH}=60\%)$ | Psychrometric conversion at current $T_{in}$ |
| $C_{in}$ | — | $1000\ \text{ppm}$ | One-sided ceiling |
| $u$ | $0.05\ \text{m}^3\text{/s}$ | $2.0\ \text{m}^3\text{/s}$ | Hard VAV actuator limits |

Per-zone temperature setpoints are injected from a dataset at each timestep (random block schedule, seeded per zone, held for 8 hours at a time, range 20–25 °C).

**Fallback:** if OSQP returns a non-solved status, `_fallback_control()` runs a proportional controller based on temperature, CO₂, and humidity errors. The fallback also respects the slew-rate limit (`u_prev ± du_max`) to avoid discontinuous flow commands even in degraded mode.

## Execution Loop

At every sampling interval $\Delta t$ (one EnergyPlus timestep):

1. EKF update yields $(x_0, d_0)$ — see updated EKF notes.
2. Read AHU supply $(T_s, W_s, C_s)$ from system state (floored at physical minima).
3. Linearise dynamics → $A_d, B_d, c_d$.
4. Build prediction matrices $\Psi, \Theta, \Phi$; extract $F_{T/W/C}, g_{T/W/C}$.
5. Assemble $H, f, A_{con}, l, u$ (including centering terms).
6. Solve QP → extract $u_0^*$.
7. Apply optional EMA output filter (currently $\alpha = 1.0$, i.e. disabled) and clip to $[u_{min}, u_{max}]$.
8. Store $u_0^*$ as $u_{prev}$; convert to mass flow $u_{mass} = u \cdot \rho_{air}$.
9. Run Phase-2 Ideal-Ask QP (see updated Section 4 notes).
10. Broadcast `{ideal_temp, ideal_hum, ideal_co2, u_cmd, saturation_index}` to AHU Coordinator.
